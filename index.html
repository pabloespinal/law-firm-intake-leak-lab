02 / The operating view

A lead is not a win. It is the start of a relay.

1

Capture

Make every channel lead somewhere useful: a live answer, a short form, or an immediate booking path.

2

Respond

Measure the share of inquiries that receive a meaningful reply, not merely an automated receipt.

3

Book

Give qualified prospects one obvious next step and reduce the back-and-forth that creates drop-off.

4

Follow through

Track source, status, tasks, appointments, and outcome in one place so no inquiry becomes invisible.
